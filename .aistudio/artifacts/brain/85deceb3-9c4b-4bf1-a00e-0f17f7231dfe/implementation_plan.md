# DBMG Consulting — Corporate Web Application & Velocity RE Platform

A modern, high-precision corporate web application for **DBMG Consulting** and its specialized acquisition program **Velocity RE**, engineered with a clean executive aesthetic, rigorous typographic hierarchy, generous whitespace, and a unified brand color system.

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Presents DBMG Consulting’s enterprise operational advisory services alongside Velocity RE’s specialized private, off-market real estate acquisition advisory. Visitors can explore proprietary methodologies (ALVI Framework), examine sector-specific consulting solutions across 6 major industries, review audited case studies with quantified KPIs, and submit structured corporate inquiries.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: C-suite executives, private equity sponsors, enterprise operations directors, real estate principals, institutional investors, and high-net-worth real estate sellers seeking private off-market execution.
- **Key Value**: Establishes immediate corporate authority, demonstrates verifiable quantitative outcomes (e.g., 34% fulfillment improvement, 8.2% cap rates, $1.45M off-market waterfront placement), and provides a friction-free intake channel.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: View Architecture — Stateful Tab Routing with Browser History Sync**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Maintain active view state in React with URL hash/query sync (`#about`, `#industries`, `#velocity-re`, `#case-studies`, `#contact`).
  - *Why*: Gives the user instantaneous tab switching without full-page reload lag while preserving browser back/forward buttons and shareable deep links.
- **Decision 2: Velocity RE Sub-Branding Distinction**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Apply a dedicated visual language (sapphire-to-velocity blue gradient, pocket-listing timeline, regulatory badge) to clearly distinguish real estate acquisition advisory from corporate operational consulting.
  - *Why*: Ensures strict compliance clarity (transactional partners, not brokers) while highlighting the specialized nature of private off-market deals.
- **Decision 3: Zero-Mock Complete Working Forms & Calculators**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Complete client-side validation, instant feedback toast/modal, and working interactive Cap Rate calculator.
  - *Why*: Eliminates dead clicks or non-functional placeholders, creating a high-fidelity enterprise experience.
